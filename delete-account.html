Account Deletion

War Cards Online

This page explains how users of War Cards Online can request deletion of their account and related personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your War Cards Online account, please send an email to:

guyzohar567@gmail.com

In your email, please include the email address used to sign in to the app and write that you are requesting account deletion.

What Data Will Be Deleted

After verifying your request, we will delete or anonymize personal account information associated with your War Cards Online account, including where applicable:

  • Account profile information
  • Email address associated with the account
  • Display name
  • Avatar and profile information
  • Friend code and friend relationships
  • Virtual coin balance and related profile data

Data That May Be Retained

Some limited data may be retained where necessary for legal, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or operational reasons. This may include anonymized gameplay records, reward claim logs, or technical records required to protect the integrity of the service.

Processing Time

We will process account deletion requests within a reasonable time after receiving and verifying the request.

Contact

If you have any questions about account deletion or privacy, contact us at:

guyzohar567@gmail.com
